## 📌 About the Project

**CareerBridge Pro** is an AI-powered job matching platform designed to connect job seekers with relevant career opportunities based on their skills, qualifications, and career goals.

The platform helps job seekers understand their strengths, explore career paths, monitor their progress, and discover suitable jobs. It also supports employers in finding candidates who match their hiring requirements.

Our goal is to make career planning and job searching more organized, personalized, and accessible.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🧠 **AI-Based Skill Analysis** — Identify strengths and skill gaps.
- 🗺️ **Personalized Career Roadmap** — Explore career goals and development paths.
- 📊 **Progress Monitoring** — Track career preparation and progress.
- 🎯 **Intelligent Job Matching** — Discover opportunities aligned with skills and qualifications.
- 🏢 **Employer Hiring Support** — Help employers find suitable candidates.
