# CareerBridge Pro
### System Analysis and Design (SAD) Project

## Project Overview
CareerBridge Pro is an AI-powered job matching platform designed to connect job seekers with suitable job opportunities based on their skills, qualifications, and career goals.

The platform aims to help job seekers identify their strengths, plan their careers, monitor progress, and find relevant job opportunities. It also supports employers in finding suitable candidates.
